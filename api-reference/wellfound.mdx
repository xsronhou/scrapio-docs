---
title: Wellfound
description: Search Wellfound (formerly AngelList Talent) startup job listings by role.
---

<Note>
  Wellfound endpoints require a paid plan (Starter or higher). Without one, these endpoints return `402 plan_upgrade_required`. See [Pricing](/pricing) for details.
</Note>

## Endpoints

- `GET /v1/wellfound/jobs` — Search startup job listings by role slug (e.g. `software-engineer`). Returns full job descriptions, compensation text, and company info; cursor-paginated.
