---
title: LinkedIn
description: Fetch a public LinkedIn Company Page's summary, post feed, and single-post detail.
---

<Note>
  LinkedIn endpoints require a paid plan (Starter or higher). Without one, these endpoints return `402 plan_upgrade_required`. See [Pricing](/pricing) for details.
</Note>

## Endpoints

- `GET /v1/linkedin/company` — Fetch a Company Page's public summary by vanity name or url.
- `GET /v1/linkedin/company/posts` — Fetch a Company Page's current post feed.
- `GET /v1/linkedin/post` — Fetch a single post's detail by url.
