---
title: Google Ad Library
description: Search Google's Ads Transparency Center and fetch single-ad detail.
---

<Note>
  Google Ad Library endpoints require a paid plan (Starter or higher). Without one, these endpoints return `402 plan_upgrade_required`. See [Pricing](/pricing) for details.
</Note>

## Endpoints

- `GET /v1/google/ad-library/search` — Search advertisers by name and list their ads.
- `GET /v1/google/ad-library/ad` — Fetch a single ad's detail by its composite ad id.
