---
title: Trustpilot
description: Fetch Trustpilot reviews for a business domain, with rating/language/verified filters.
---

<Note>
  Trustpilot endpoints require a paid plan (Starter or higher). Without one, these endpoints return `402 plan_upgrade_required`. See [Pricing](/pricing) for details.
</Note>

## Endpoints

- `GET /v1/trustpilot/reviews` — Fetch reviews for a business domain, with optional star-rating, language, and verified-only filters. Returns a business summary plus reviews, cursor-paginated.
